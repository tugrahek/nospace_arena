Provenance
The vendored font is byte-identical to the subset distributed with Poing
Studios Godot AdMob plugin v5.1.0. It contains 1,321 glyphs and 871 encoded
characters; it is not the full upstream Noto CJK font. Font name/version and
copyright are retained in its metadata. All 871 encoded character outlines
were compared with, and match, the official upstream Regular font below.
